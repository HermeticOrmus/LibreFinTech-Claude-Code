# Pantry queue: LibreFinTech-Claude-Code

## How this fills

1. Read the latest competitor map, X mine and people mine.
2. Propose 5 to 8 Goal atoms that answer their themes. The Menu needs at least 3.
3. Each atom needs a Done predicate someone else can check on this repo, a surface, the evidence rows it answers, and a confidence (high, medium or low).
4. Save as `YYYY-MM-DD-pantry-queue.md`; the Menu reads the newest one.
5. Retire an atom only with a bullet under "Explicitly not stocked" of the form `<Title>: shipped, PR #N` or `<Title>: parked, <reason>`.

## Explicitly not stocked (and why)

- An MCP server of our own for a payment provider. Not stocked: Stripe, PayPal, Plaid, Adyen, Square and Razorpay ship official ones (Map), so the `provider-mcp` atom points at them instead.
- A ledger engine. Not stocked: Formance, Blnk and TigerBeetle exist (Map); the `ledger-schema` template is a reference schema, not an engine.
- Jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction compliance content that reads as legal interpretation. Not stocked: the pack is engineering guidance, and legal and compliance decisions stay with the operator's compliance function (README Disclaimer).
- Anything that needs a live provider account, real money or paid services to verify. Every Done predicate above runs locally or against sandbox keys.
